# Privacy Policy for TruWord

**Last updated: January 25, 2026**

## Overview

TruWord ("we", "our", or "the app") is a daily Bible devotional app designed to help you grow in faith through scripture reading, reflection, and prayer. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

**Important:** TruWord does not require account creation. Your personal reflections and journal entries are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.

## Data We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide and improve the app experience:

- **Purchase Information**: When you make an in-app purchase, transaction data is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Play Store). We receive confirmation of your purchase status but do not have access to your payment details.
- **Anonymous Usage Analytics**: We may collect anonymous, aggregated data about how the app is used (e.g., which features are popular) to improve the user experience. This data cannot identify you personally.
- **Crash Reports**: If the app crashes, anonymous diagnostic information may be collected to help us fix bugs and improve stability.
- **Device Information**: Basic device information (device type, OS version) may be collected for compatibility and troubleshooting purposes.

## Data Stored on Your Device

The following data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to our servers:

- **Your Name**: The name you enter during onboarding for personalization.
- **Journal Entries**: Your personal reflections, prayers, and gratitude notes.
- **Preferences**: Your spiritual goals, notification settings, and reminder times.
- **Progress Data**: Your reading streaks, daily progress, and favorite verses.
- **Premium Status**: Whether you have purchased lifetime access.

**Important:** This data remains on your device. If you uninstall the app, this data will be permanently deleted.

## Third-Party Services

TruWord uses the following third-party services:

- **RevenueCat**: Manages in-app purchases and subscription status. [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)
- **Expo**: Development framework for building the app. [Expo Privacy Policy](https://expo.dev/privacy)
- **Apple App Store / Google Play Store**: Handles app distribution and payment processing. See their respective privacy policies.

These services may collect their own data as described in their privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share anonymous, aggregated data with:
- Analytics providers to understand app usage patterns
- Service providers who assist in operating our app

We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of our users.

## Children's Privacy

TruWord is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Since the app does not require account creation and stores all personal data locally on the device, children can use the app safely. Parents and guardians can manage app usage through their device's parental controls.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

## Contact

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at: [info@eternt.com](mailto:info@eternt.com)
