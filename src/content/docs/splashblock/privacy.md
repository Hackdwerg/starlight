# Privacy Policy for Splash Block

**Last updated: January 10, 2026**

## Overview

Splash Block ("the App") is a puzzle game developed by eternt. We respect your privacy and are committed to transparency about our data practices.

## Data We Collect

The App does not require an account or collect personal information such as your name, email, or phone number.

However, the App uses third-party services that may collect certain data:

- **Device Identifiers**: Anonymous device IDs used for advertising and analytics
- **Purchase History**: Records of in-app purchases (processed by Apple and RevenueCat)
- **Usage Data**: App interaction data for analytics purposes
- **Crash Data**: Technical data to improve app stability

## Third-Party Services

- **Google AdMob**: Displays advertisements. [AdMob Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **RevenueCat**: Manages in-app purchases. [RevenueCat Privacy Policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy)

## Children's Privacy

The App is designed to be safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads shown are configured for child-safe content (COPPA compliant).

## Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is only shared with the third-party services listed above for advertising and purchase management.
